## Course Description

Object-Oriented Programming introduces programming from an
object-oriented perspective and develops the competencies needed to
design maintainable software for data science applications. The course
covers classes and objects, abstraction, encapsulation, inheritance,
polymorphism, special methods and operator overloading, exceptions,
advanced class features, packaging, testing, distribution,
documentation, and project organization.

Through lectures and guided laboratory work, students design, implement,
test, debug, package, and present object-oriented Python applications
using modern software engineering and design principles.

# Ethical Use of AI

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Guideline                           Policy
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  **Permitted Use**                   AI tools may assist with
                                      brainstorming, improving writing
                                      clarity, or supporting data
                                      analysis.

  **Disclosure**                      AI use must be properly disclosed
                                      in references or through an AI
                                      acknowledgement.

  **Student Responsibility**          Students are responsible for
                                      verifying the accuracy,
                                      originality, and ethical integrity
                                      of AI-assisted outputs.

  **Prohibited Use**                  AI must not generate entire
                                      assignments, research papers, or
                                      examination answers.

  **Academic Integrity**              Plagiarism, data fabrication, and
                                      over-reliance on AI without
                                      critical evaluation are treated as
                                      academic integrity violations.
